### How firm should it be?

Firmness is personal, but a simple guide helps:

- **Side sleepers:** soft to medium. The mattress should cushion shoulders and hips.
- **Back sleepers:** medium to medium-firm for a neutral spine.
- **Stomach sleepers:** firm, to stop the hips sinking.

A good night's sleep comes from matching the surface to how you actually sleep, not from picking the thickest mattress.

### Mattress thickness and layers explained

Thickness is often mistaken for quality, but what matters is what is inside. A 20 cm mattress with dense foam and a solid support layer will outperform a 30 cm one filled with cheap padding. Look for three things:

- **A support base** (high-density foam or springs) that stops sagging.
- **A comfort layer** (memory foam, latex or soft foam) that shapes the feel.
- **A quality cover** that is breathable, removable if possible, and easy to keep clean.

Heavier sleepers generally benefit from thicker, denser builds. Lighter sleepers often feel more comfortable on softer, thinner comfort layers.

### Mattress or mattress topper?

If your current bed is still structurally sound but feels too firm, a topper can give you an extra comfort layer for much less than a replacement. If it sags, has visible dips, or your back aches in the morning, replace the mattress instead. A topper cannot fix a worn-out support core.

### Mattresses for kids, guest rooms and rentals

Children grow quickly, so a firm, supportive single mattress with a washable cover is a sensible choice. For guest rooms and rentals, a durable medium-firm mattress suits most people and keeps complaints low. For a master bedroom, spend more on comfort and support, because you will use it every night for years.

### Common mistakes when you buy a mattress

1. **Choosing by price alone.** A cheap mattress that sags in a year costs more than a better one that lasts a decade.
2. **Ignoring sleep position.** Side sleepers on a very firm bed get shoulder and hip pain.
3. **Forgetting the base.** A worn or unsupportive bed base ruins even a premium mattress. Use a slatted base with gaps no wider than about 7 cm.
4. **Skipping the protector.** Spills and humidity shorten a mattress's life and can void warranties.
5. **Not measuring.** Always check frame dimensions and the door and lift access for delivery.

### How to care for your mattress

1. Use a waterproof, breathable protector from day one.
2. Rotate it every three months (flip only if it is double-sided).
3. Air the room regularly and keep the bed base slatted or ventilated.
4. Vacuum the surface twice a year and spot-clean spills straight away.

With care, a quality mattress lasts around 8–10 years.
