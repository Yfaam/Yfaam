## Intro
<p>A super king mattress in the UAE measures <strong>200 × 200 cm</strong>. That's 20 cm wider than a <a href="/collections/king-mattresses">king (180 × 200 cm)</a> at the same length, and it's the difference between sharing a bed and actually having room in it. It's the size most people ask us for in master bedrooms, from Dubai villas to larger apartments in Abu Dhabi and Sharjah.</p>
<p>Every mattress on this page comes in 200 × 200 cm: pocket-spring <a href="/collections/hotel-collection-mattresses">Hotel Collection</a> models with a tight top or euro top, gel memory foam for people who sleep hot, and natural latex.</p>
<p><strong>Will it fit your room?</strong> Allow roughly 60–70 cm of walking space on each side and at the foot of the bed. With a bed frame, that usually means a wall of about 3.4 m or more. Check lift, corridor and door widths as well: our team carries the mattress to the room of your choice, but it has to fit through the door.</p>
<p><strong>Delivery and trial.</strong> Free delivery across all seven emirates, typically in 2–3 working days, and orders placed before 4pm are dispatched the same working day. Every mattress includes a 100-night sleep trial, and you can split the cost into 4 payments with Tabby or Tamara.</p>
