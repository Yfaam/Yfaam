## Intro
<p>In the UAE, "medical mattress" usually means a <strong>firm, supportive mattress</strong> that keeps the spine level instead of letting you sink in. That's what you'll find here: firm pocket-spring mattresses with high-density foam edges, from 90 × 200 to 200 × 200 cm.</p>
<p>A medical mattress isn't a medical device, and no mattress treats a health condition. If you have back, neck or joint pain, ask your doctor or physiotherapist what kind of support they recommend, then choose the firmness that matches. Our Hotel Collection Tight Top is rated 9/10 firm. For a slightly softer surface with the same support, see our <a href="/collections/orthopedic-mattresses">orthopaedic mattresses</a>.</p>
