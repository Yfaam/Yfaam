## Intro
<p>A mattress topper is the quickest way to change how your bed feels without replacing the mattress. Ours are <strong>5 cm, 300-thread-count microfibre toppers</strong> that soften a mattress that's become too firm and add a plush, hotel-bed layer on top. An elasticated skirt keeps them in place.</p>
<p>Pick the width that matches your mattress: 120 cm single, 150 cm small double, 160 cm double/queen, 180 cm king or 200 cm super king. In UAE humidity, pair it with a waterproof <a href="/collections/mattress-protectors">mattress protector</a> to keep sweat and spills out of both.</p>
<p>Delivered across the UAE in 2–3 working days, free on orders over AED 500. Pay in 4 with Tabby or Tamara.</p>
<p><strong>Topper or new mattress?</strong> A topper changes the feel of the surface, not the support underneath. It's the right fix when a mattress is in good shape but feels too firm, or when you're renting and can't replace the mattress. If yours sags or you can feel the springs, a <a href="/collections/mattresses">new mattress</a> is the better buy.</p>
