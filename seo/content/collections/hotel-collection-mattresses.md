## FAQs
What is a hotel collection mattress?|It's a mattress built to hotel specification: an individually wrapped pocket-spring core, reinforced edges and a durable quilted top. It gives the supportive, even feel you remember from a good hotel bed.
What's the difference between a tight top and a euro top?|A tight top has a quilted surface sewn flush to the mattress for a firmer, even feel; ours is rated 9/10. A euro top adds a plush layer over the springs for a softer first touch (8/10) with the same support underneath.
Which hotel mattress is best for back sleepers?|Back and front sleepers usually prefer the firmer Hotel Collection Tight Top. Side sleepers usually prefer the Euro Top or the Signature pillow top. If you have a medical condition, ask your doctor what support you need.
Do you supply hotels and serviced apartments?|Yes. The FR Tight Top and FR Euro Top use fire-retardant materials for properties with fire-safety requirements, and we take bulk orders. Message us on WhatsApp for a quote.
What sizes does the Hotel Collection come in?|From 90 × 200 cm single to 200 × 200 cm <a href="/collections/super-king-mattresses">super king</a>, including 120, 150, 160 and 180 × 200 cm.
Can I try a hotel mattress at home?|Yes. Every mattress has a 100-night sleep trial. Sleep on it for at least 21 nights, and if it isn't right we'll collect it for a refund or one-time exchange (AED 250 collection charge). See the <a href="/pages/returns-policy">returns policy</a>.
