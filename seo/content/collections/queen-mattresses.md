## Intro
<p>In the UAE a queen size mattress is usually <strong>150 × 200 cm</strong>, and many bed frames here also take <strong>160 × 200 cm</strong>. We stock both. A queen is the practical choice for a second bedroom, a smaller master bedroom or a single sleeper who wants room to stretch.</p>
<p>Choose from pocket-spring <a href="/collections/hotel-collection-mattresses">Hotel Collection</a> models, cooling gel memory foam and natural latex, all in queen sizes. Measure the inside of your bed frame before you order: 150 and 160 cm frames aren't interchangeable.</p>
<p><strong>Delivery and trial.</strong> Free delivery across the UAE, typically in 2–3 working days. Every mattress has a 100-night sleep trial, and you can pay in 4 with Tabby or Tamara.</p>
<p><strong>Which feel?</strong> Back and front sleepers usually prefer the firmer Hotel Collection tight top. Side sleepers usually prefer a euro top or gel memory foam. Add a matching 150 or 160 cm <a href="/collections/mattress-toppers">topper</a> and a <a href="/collections/mattress-protectors">mattress protector</a>, sized to the same width.</p>
## FAQs
What size is a queen mattress in the UAE?|A queen mattress in the UAE is usually 150 × 200 cm. Some frames sold here as queen or double take 160 × 200 cm, and we stock both.
Queen vs king: which is right for me?|A queen (150 × 200 cm) suits one sleeper or a smaller room. For two adults, a <a href="/collections/king-mattresses">king (180 × 200 cm)</a> gives each person 90 cm.
Will a 160x200 mattress fit a 150x200 bed frame?|No. A 160 cm mattress won't sit flat in a 150 cm frame, and a 150 cm mattress will slide around in a 160 cm frame. Measure the inside of your frame first.
What size topper fits a queen mattress?|Choose the 150 cm <a href="/collections/mattress-toppers">topper</a> for a 150 × 200 cm mattress, or the 160 cm topper for 160 × 200 cm.
Can I return a queen mattress?|Yes. Every mattress has a 100-night sleep trial: sleep on it for at least 21 nights, and if it isn't right we'll collect it for a refund or one-time exchange. An AED 250 collection charge applies. See the <a href="/pages/returns-policy">returns policy</a>.
