## Intro
<p>An orthopaedic (or orthopedic) mattress gives firm, even support so your hips and shoulders don't sink out of line with your spine. Ours use an individually wrapped pocket-spring core with high-density foam edges, in every UAE size from 90 × 200 to 200 × 200 cm.</p>
<p>Choose your level of firmness. The Hotel Collection <strong>Tight Top</strong> (9/10) is the firmest and suits back and front sleepers. The <strong>Euro Top</strong> (8/10) adds a plush layer for side sleepers who still want firm support underneath. For the very firmest options, see <a href="/collections/medical-mattresses">medical mattresses</a>.</p>
<p>A mattress isn't a treatment, so if you have a back or joint condition, ask your doctor or physiotherapist what they recommend. Every mattress includes free UAE delivery and a 100-night sleep trial.</p>
<p><strong>Not sure how firm to go?</strong> If you sleep on your side, a surface that's too hard can press on the shoulder and hip. That's where the euro top helps. Use the 100-night trial: give it at least 21 nights, because your body takes a few weeks to adjust.</p>
